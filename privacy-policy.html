This Privacy Policy explains how Medical Cannabis Cup Diary collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, information associated with your account may include your username, email address, date of birth, location information you choose to provide, profile information, account settings and authentication information.

The app may also store content and activity that you choose to create, including diary entries, reviews, ratings, comments, uploaded images, reports and other information submitted through the service.

2. Sign-In Providers

Medical Cannabis Cup Diary may allow you to sign in using third-party authentication providers such as Facebook or Google. When you use a third-party sign-in provider, we receive the information required to authenticate your account, subject to the permissions you grant and that provider's own privacy practices.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create, authenticate and maintain your account.
  • Provide the app's diary, review and community features.
  • Display profile and user-generated content where appropriate.
  • Maintain app security and prevent abuse.
  • Investigate reports and enforce community rules.
  • Provide account, moderation and service notifications.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the service.

4. User-Generated Content

Some information you submit may be visible to other users where you choose to use public or community features. Information intended to remain private, such as private diary information, is not made public simply because you have an account.

5. Service Providers

Medical Cannabis Cup Diary uses third-party technology and infrastructure providers to operate features such as authentication, data storage, notifications and other app services. These providers may process information as necessary to provide those services.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Account information may be removed when an account is deleted, subject to information that must legitimately be retained.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their account through the account deletion controls provided within Medical Cannabis Cup Diary.

Further information is available on our Account & Data Deletion page.

8. Children's Privacy

Medical Cannabis Cup Diary is intended for adults. Registration requires users to confirm that they are at least 18 years old and meet the app's eligibility requirements.

9. Medical Information

Medical Cannabis Cup Diary is a tracking and community application. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, prescribing services or healthcare. Users should seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional regarding medical decisions.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled by the service. No electronic system can, however, guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, its services or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy or personal information can be directed to the Medical Cannabis Cup Diary developer through the support contact provided within the app.
