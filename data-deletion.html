Medical Cannabis Cup Diary provides users with the ability to delete their account and associated account data from within the app.

Delete Your Account

  1. Open Medical Cannabis Cup Diary.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Read the deletion information and confirm the deletion when prompted.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

The account deletion process removes the account through the app's dedicated deletion workflow and signs the user out when deletion has completed.

Data associated with the account is removed where applicable, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

Facebook Login

If you created or accessed your Medical Cannabis Cup Diary account using Facebook Login, you can still delete your Medical Cannabis Cup Diary account using the steps above.

Removing Medical Cannabis Cup Diary from your Facebook account does not replace the in-app account deletion process if you also want your Medical Cannabis Cup Diary account and associated app data deleted.

Unable to Access Your Account?

If you cannot access the app or your account and therefore cannot use the in-app deletion option, contact the Medical Cannabis Cup Diary developer using the support contact provided for the app.
